# Payment method badges

Used by the homepage payment methods strip (`src/components/home/PaymentMethods.jsx`). The logos
are trademarks of their owners and are shown only to say that Duoly accepts these payment methods.

- **Datatrans Payment Logos** (https://github.com/datatrans/payment-logos), licensed
  CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), copied unchanged:
  visa (`visa-alt.svg`), mastercard, amex (`american-express.svg`), apple-pay, google-pay,
  paypal, klarna, amazon-pay, ideal, bancontact, alipay, eps, przelewy24, blik, twint, mobilepay,
  wechat-pay, wero, sepa.
- **Adyen checkout logos** (https://checkoutshopper-live.adyen.com/checkoutshopper/images/logos/),
  published by Adyen for showing payment methods at checkout: revolut-pay (`revolutpay.svg`),
  mb-way (`mbway.svg`), satispay.
- **Wikimedia Commons, File:Bizum.svg** (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bizum.svg),
  public domain; placed on a white 120×80 badge to match the others: bizum.
